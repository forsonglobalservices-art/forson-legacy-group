About Forson Legacy Group

Forson Legacy Group brings together business activities that serve people, support livelihoods and contribute to a cleaner, more productive future.

From digital tools that support school administration to agricultural trading, recycling and distribution, our focus is on dependable service, strong partnerships and long-term value.
